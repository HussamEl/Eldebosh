How we pick products

Updated 2026-01-01

We do not test products physically

We never claim to have tested a product we have not used. This site is built on documented specifications from manufacturers and retailers, compared against each other and put in context.

What a recommendation is based on

  • Published specifications from the manufacturer or the retailer product page.
  • How those specifications answer a concrete everyday problem.
  • Comparison against alternatives in the same price range.

What we never do

  • We do not invent ratings, scores or stars.
  • We do not invent test results or measurements.
  • We do not publish figures we could not check against a source.

Prices

We do not list fixed prices, because they change quickly. Instead we state a price band — budget, mid-range or premium — and the date the data was last checked.
